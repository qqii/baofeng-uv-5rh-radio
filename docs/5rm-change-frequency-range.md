# 5RM Change Frequency Range

This is a text-only conversion of `res/5RM Change Frequency Range.pdf`. Images from the PDF are intentionally omitted.

1. Open the CPS application: `T6UV Series EN CPS+`.
2. Choose the correct serial port.
3. Read the 5RM radio.
4. Open `Tools` > `Frequency Range`.
5. Choose the correct serial port again, then write.
6. Choose the frequency range you want from the model dropdown, then write.

For this project, the target range is the factory/full-range option shown by the CPS as:

`UHF: 400-520MHz, VHF: 136-174MHz, VHF2: 200-260MHz`
